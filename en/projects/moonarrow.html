MoonArrow Studios

A community for people who make games: a forum to show progress and a marketplace to share assets.

View the code ↗ (opens in a new tab)
Year
2025, 12th-grade final project (PAP)
Role
Solo, start to finish
Result
Graded 20/20
Stack
PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap, JavaScript

The challenge

People starting out in game development spread their questions across several Discord servers and hunt for free assets on dozens of different sites. I wanted to bring both into one place, built for that community.

What I built

  • A forum with categories, hashtags, votes, comments and view counts.
  • A marketplace to publish and download 3D models, textures, music and videos.
  • Profiles with followers, a feed of the people you follow and email notifications.
  • An admin panel to manage users, posts, assets, comments, categories and reports.
  • Sign-up, password recovery and account deletion, all confirmed by email.

More screens

How it works

PHP without a framework, using PDO and prepared statements, on a 17-table MySQL database. Post HTML goes through HTMLPurifier before it is saved, and emails are sent with PHPMailer. The interface uses Bootstrap 5 and JavaScript.

What I learned

It was the first project where I had to think about everything: the data model, the permissions of each kind of user, form security and the moderator experience. Over 224 commits I learned how to organise a project that keeps growing.
