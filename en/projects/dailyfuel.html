DailyFuel

A private diary for calories, weight and water, with reminders to drink water and work out.

The challenge

I wanted to gain weight and didn't know whether I was eating enough or drinking water regularly. I needed to see all of it in one place, every day, so I built my own app.

What I built

  • A meal diary with calories and macronutrients.
  • Weight tracking with a progress chart.
  • A water counter for the day.
  • Phone reminders to drink water and work out.
  • Workouts, habits and a to-do list.

More screens

How it works

Next.js with TypeScript, exported as a static site and hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Supabase handles email sign-in and stores each account's data, with row-level security so nobody can read anyone else's data. Notifications are delivered through ntfy.

What I learned

TypeScript and React in a real project, and how to migrate the data from an old version to a new database without losing anything.
